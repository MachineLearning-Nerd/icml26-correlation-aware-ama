# Claim 3 — general DSIC preservation

**Verdict: VERIFIED (HIGH confidence).**

The extra payment `pCor_i(V_-i)` is identical under truthful reporting and any
own-value misreport, so it cancels exactly from the utility difference. This
structural identity holds for arbitrary allocation menus, positive weights,
boosts, rival-only functions, bidders, values, and reports.

The independent checker adds randomized feasible multi-item menus and 800
misreports. Maximum utility gain is nonpositive. The negative control makes the
payment depend on the bidder's own report and exhibits a profitable gain of
1.0, so the checker is sensitive to the claimed restriction.
