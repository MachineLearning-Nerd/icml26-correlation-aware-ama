The CA-AMA payment adds `p_i^Cor(V_{-i})`, which is constant with respect to
bidder `i`'s report. For every fixed rival profile and any truthful or
misreported value, this surcharge cancels exactly from the utility
difference. Therefore every weakly dominant truthful-report inequality of the
underlying AMA is preserved for any number of bidders and items.

The independent checker evaluates the symbolic cancellation and exhaustive
finite multi-item property cases; deliberately adding own-report dependence
is the negative control and produces a profitable deviation.

**Assessment: VERIFIED.** This is a general mechanism-level argument, not an
empirical two-bidder misreport sample.
